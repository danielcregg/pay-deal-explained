The nine pay rises

The draft raises pay nine times between September 2026 and March 2029. Here is your salary climbing, one rise at a time.

3

Flat sum or percentage?

Five of the rises come with a minimum payment. Whether you get the minimum or the percentage depends on your salary, and the dividing line, the lower-pay threshold, is €60,000.

4

Why 10.75% comes to about 11.3%

The headline adds the percentages together. Your pay grows by a little more, because each rise is worked out on pay that has already gone up. That is compounding.

5

Your back pay

The first rise is backdated to 1 September 2026. If the deal is accepted, the back pay owed since then arrives as a lump sum.
