Which demos should the site have?

1

The nine pay rises (animated)

Your salary climbing step by step from September 2026 to March 2029, like the sketch above.

2

Flat sum or percentage? (interactive)

Drag a salary to see which rule you get at each step, and where the €60k threshold comes in.

3

Why 10.75% comes to about 11.3% (animated)

Each rise lands on pay that has already gone up. A short animation shows the compounding.

4

My pay calculator

Pick your grade and point or type a salary, set full-time or part-time (including hourly), and see every increase in euro.

5

Back pay estimate

Roughly how much is owed from 1 September and could arrive in January if the deal is accepted.

6

Pay vs prices (chart)

Your pay against the Central Bank's inflation forecasts, with a slider to try your own inflation figure.

7

How we got here (animated timeline)

The old deal expiring in June, the work-to-rule, the 14 October strike, the 3.30am deal, the December ballots.

8

This deal vs the last one

10.25% over 30 months (2024–26) next to 10.75% over 33 months, compared on pace and on who gains most.

9

Plain-English glossary

Local bargaining, review clause, general round, lower-pay threshold, explained in a sentence or two each.

Sources: draft deal terms as reported by RTÉ, The Irish Times and the Irish Independent (11 Nov 2026); salary scales from TU-IOT Circular 002/2026 (from 1 June 2026). The sketches treat local bargaining as a 1% rise for everyone and show gross pay before increments.
