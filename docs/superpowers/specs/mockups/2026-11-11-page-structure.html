Ground rules (my calls)

  • Neutral: explains the deal and takes no side in the ballot.
  • Private: your salary never leaves your device. No tracking and no cookies.
  • Honest numbers: gross pay, increments excluded, assumptions shown next to every result.
  • Easy to update when the official text comes out or the ballot result lands.
  • Respects reduced motion: animations switch off for anyone who has asked their device to reduce motion.
